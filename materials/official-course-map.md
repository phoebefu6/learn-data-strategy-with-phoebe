# Official course map - learn-data-strategy-with-phoebe ("Data Strategy for CDOs")

Internal build document. Never linked from an audience page. Written 2026-10-29 by the foundations
lead, before any page. Every number a page quotes must appear here with its tier.

Audience: new and aspiring CDOs, CDAOs and Heads of Data who must write a first data strategy in
roughly 90 days. Single track, 6 sessions x 45 min. Running case: **Keelson Mutual, a CONSTRUCTED
mid-size insurer** (name grep-free across the estate on 2026-10-29), labelled constructed on every
page. No real insurer in the case, no invented market statistic.

## Evidence tiers

- **AT SOURCE** - read on the publisher's own page during this build (2026-10-29).
- **REPORTED** - could not be read at source (paywall, 403, book); stated only as "reported" with
  where it was reported, or cut.
- **CONSTRUCTED** - Keelson Mutual and every Keelson number; teaching material, never a measurement.

| F4 | Same article, cross-industry figures as printed: less than half of an organisation's structured data is actively used in making decisions; less than 1% of its unstructured data is analysed or used at all; more than 70% of employees have access to data they should not; 80% of analysts' time is spent discovering and preparing data. | AT SOURCE (as printed in the article's opening; the underlying studies are not named on the readable page, so pages say "the article reports") | HBR |
| F5 | Defense = minimising downside risk (compliance, security, privacy, integrity), tied to a single source of truth (SSOT); offense = supporting business objectives such as revenue, profitability and customer satisfaction, tied to multiple versions of the truth (MVOTs) built from the SSOT; the balance shifts with industry, regulation and competition. | REPORTED (body behind paywall; consistent across secondary summaries) | HBR 2017 via summaries |
| F6 | UK National Data Strategy, Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport, published 9 September 2020 (page updated 9 December 2020). | F7 | US Federal Data Strategy: mission statement, 10 principles (in three groups: ethical governance, conscious design, learning culture), 40 practices (three groups: culture 1-10, governing/managing/protecting 11-26, efficient and appropriate use 27-40), and annual action plans (2020 and 2021 listed). ## Bench canon (session 3) - python and node agree exactly

Data: 24 CONSTRUCTED Keelson use cases in `assets/bets-data.js` (generated by
`materials/build-bets-data.py`). Engine: `assets/bets-live.js`. Reference: `materials/bets-reference.py`
(separate implementation). Node runner: `materials/bets-node.js`. On 2026-10-29 the two canons were
compared as parsed JSON: IDENTICAL (every id list, integer, and score to 3 decimals).

Defaults: weights value 0.5, feasibility 0.2, readiness 0.3; budget 1,100 (thousands, constructed);
capacity 24 team-months; readiness gate 40. Score per bet 0-100; a set's score is the sum of three.

| Rung | Pick | Value (sum of bands) | Cost | Team-months | Offense:defense | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 value alone | PEL, FNW, PHO | 28 | 2,110 | 41 | 3:0 | all 3 under the readiness gate; PEL and FNW missing their SCV dependency; does not fit |
| 2 + feasibility | SEV, PEL, FNW | 28 | 1,710 | 34 | 3:0 | all 3 under the gate; PEL and FNW missing SCV; does not fit |
| 3 + readiness (gated) | CFT, FTR, SUB | 23 | 930 | 20 | 3:0 | fits, but CFT and SUB need CDQ first |

| 5 rank stability | exact set survives 173 of 500 draws (34.6%) | | | | | inclusion: FTR 352, CDQ 321, CFT 321, CAT 179, RET 179, SUB 148 (of 500) |

Frontier (value against effort, all 24): LTR, GLO, SUB, SEV, PEL.
Self-test on load: 4 of 4 properties pass; 3 of 3 planted faults (no budget check, no dependency
check, no gate) are caught.

Honest reading for pages: the bench claims ORDERINGS only. Greedy carries one more value band than
exact (22 against 21) and still scores lower, because the score also weights feasibility and
readiness; say that, do not hide it. The stability rung is the headline: one bet (FTR) is in the
exact set in 352 of 500 draws; the other two slots move. Tell the board the range.

## Not covered (honest list)

- NPV, IRR, ROI maths and business-case templates (planned business-cases course).
- Selling or licensing data, data marketplaces (planned data monetisation course).
- Governance in depth: policies, stewardship programmes, metadata tooling, maturity models (gov shelf).
- Architecture choices (lakehouse, mesh tooling, vendors), cloud cost.
- Hiring, job descriptions, compensation; org redesign for AI.
- Any real insurer's strategy, market share or loss ratio. Any claim about CDO tenure.
- Sector regulation detail (Solvency II, IFRS 17, NAIC): mentioned only as "regulated", never taught.
- Phoebe's private CDAIO notes shaped structure only; nothing is cited from them.
