Learn Data Strategy with Phoebe

For new and aspiring chief data officers, CDAOs and heads of data who have about 90 days to write a first data strategy. Six sessions in the order the job actually runs: say what is going on before promising anything, decide what the data is for, pick the first three bets, settle who delivers them, fund them and track value, then put the whole strategy on one page. One constructed running case, an insurer called Keelson Mutual, and one bench: 24 candidate bets scored, fitted to a budget and a team, and stress-tested in your browser.

The six sessions 🧭 · from the first conversation to the strategy on a page

The running case is Keelson Mutual, a constructed mid-size insurer: regulated, running on legacy policy and claims systems, with claims, pricing, fraud and customer teams all asking for data work. Maya Okafor has just joined as its first chief data officer and has promised the board a strategy in 90 days. Each session adds one page to Keelson's strategy. Keelson and everyone in it are invented for teaching, and every page says so.

🩺 Session 1 · foundations

Diagnosis before ambition

Rumelt's kernel applied to data, a 30-day listening tour, the four ways data strategies fail, and Keelson's one-paragraph diagnosis written from interview notes.

▶ Start here 🎯 Session 2 · core

What the data is for

Offense and defense, one source of truth and many fit-for-purpose versions, and where a regulated insurer sits on the dial. Keelson's ambition statement.

the dial 🎲 Session 3 · bench night

The first three bets

24 constructed bets scored on value, feasibility and data readiness, fitted to a budget and a team, exact search beside greedy, the cost of quick wins first, and how stable the pick is.

★ the bench 🏛️ Session 4 · deeper

Operating model

Centralised, hub and spoke or federated; where the CDO reports; who owns each bet's data; and the minimum governance that lets three bets ship.

who delivers 💷 Session 5 · deeper

Funding and value tracking

Central budget, chargeback or staged bet funding; leading signals before lagging ones; and the board ask. Two value metrics for each of Keelson's bets.

the board ask 📜 Session 6 · capstone

The strategy on a page

Published national data strategies read as worked material, sequencing, risks and a comms cadence. Keelson's strategy on one page and its 100-day roadmap.
